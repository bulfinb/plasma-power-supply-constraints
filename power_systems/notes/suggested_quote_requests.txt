suggested_quote_requests.txt - vendor quotes that would give current, well-defined supply costs
Prepared by Claude (AI) on 2026-11-10 at BB's request. To be sent by BB; not yet sent.

Why: published supply costs are sparse, old or unclear about what is included (see the capex table in
notes/supply_maturity_niche.md). A budgetary quote states the date, currency and scope, so it can go
directly onto a cost-per-kW figure.

For every quote, ask for:
  - budgetary (non-binding) price, currency and date
  - price with and without the power supply, and what else is included (isolator/circulator,
    tuner, controls, cooling, installation)
  - output power basis (CW output at the waveguide flange) and wall-plug efficiency at rated power
  - tube/component lifetime and replacement cost
  - whether the price may be cited in a publication (and in what form, e.g. "vendor quote, 2026")

When a quote arrives: register it under the next PW ID, save it in source_archive/<PW ID>/, tell Claude to hash and register it,
and record any confidentiality terms in source_register.csv.
