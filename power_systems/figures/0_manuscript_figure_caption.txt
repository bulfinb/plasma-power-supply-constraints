Published power supplies against output frequency. (a) Output power of a single supply unit (rated or family maximum); lines show stated frequency bands. Installed systems built from several units are larger: induction-heating supplies of 16-42 MW and an installation of 126 MW, a 1.76 MW precipitator installation of 32 high-voltage DC units, and 915 MHz microwave containers of several MW. (b) Published efficiencies (points) and the ranges adopted for the projections (boxes; for K2, the 100-400 kHz range used for the ICP torch). Efficiency is from mains input to generator output unless marked; values for the tube or amplifier alone are not shown (Supporting Information 1). DC supplies are drawn side by side in the shaded band (A1 arc-torch supplies, J1 rectifiers, D1 high-voltage DC). Grey: classes not used in the projections (R1 RF tube oscillator, R2 solid-state RF, M3 solid-state microwave, drawn just right of 2.45 GHz; H1 klystron). Projected values are not shown. Nanosecond pulsed supplies (P1) have no carrier frequency and are not shown. Data and sources: power_systems/supply_values.csv.
