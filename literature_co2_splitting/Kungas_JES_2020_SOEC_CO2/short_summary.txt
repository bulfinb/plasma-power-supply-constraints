Küngas (2020) - SOEC CO2 electrolysis to CO (benchmark), Topsoe stacks, 8250 cm2, 750 C
Source ID: SRC025. J. Electrochem. Soc. 167, 044508. Review by a Topsoe author; SOEC data
are the company's own stack tests (refs 57, 61, 62). Published version.

Scale: 8250 cm2 stacks; 200-640 mA/cm2 in the reported tests (up to ~770 in
Fig. 4). Commercial context: 12 Nm3/h CO demo (2016), two 96 Nm3/h eCOs
plants contracted for 2020. No plant-level data (author's statement).

Energy (stack DC electricity, Eq. 16; heat and balance of plant excluded):
- EPC 2.5 kWh/Nm3 CO at 200 mA/cm2 (Table I; the text's 1.13 V gives 2.70);
  2.96 -> 3.06 over a 2000 h test at 450 mA/cm2 (1.24 -> 1.28 V);
  ~3.3 -> ~3.6 over a 7600 h system test at 640 mA/cm2 (figure).
  = 202-290 kJ/mol CO, i.e. 71-103% of dH (283 kJ/mol) as electricity.
- Stacks run below the thermoneutral voltage (1.465 V) at the lower
  current densities, so the remaining reaction energy enters as heat;
  its amount and the heater power are not reported.
- Theoretical minimum 2.3 kWh/Nm3 at 800 C. FE 100% (chromatography).
- For comparison, plasma (Kiefer 2024, SRC016) best wall-plug 19.6
  kWh/Nm3 CO; not like for like (wall-plug vs stack DC, heat excluded).

Conversion: not reported; single-pass utilisation and CO purity are needed
from another source.

Product: O2 leaves at the anode, so CO and O2 are separated in the cell;
cathode product is CO + unconverted CO2, free of H2/H2O.

Duration: 2000 h (2.2 %V/1000 h) and 7600 h (1.2-1.3 %V/1000 h).

Reporting quality (benchmark R1-R4): measured stack data, second-hand;
boundary stack DC only; basis partly stated; not independent of the vendor.
Conversion/product results lower confidence (not reported); energy results
moderate confidence (consistent and reproducible, but narrow boundary).
