Hecimovic et al. (2023) - 2.45 GHz microwave torch with water-cooled effluent channels, ~1 bar, <=2.7 kW
Source ID: SRC017. J. CO2 Util. 71, 102473. Published version + ESI.
Experimental. Pure CO2, no catalyst. Same torch as SRC016 (Kiefer 2024): count once.

Scale: absorbed microwave power 0.8-2.7 kW; CO2 4-20 ls/min (20 C,
1 atm; = SRC016's slm at 0 C x 1.073). Supply efficiency and auxiliaries
out of scope; wall-plug values for this torch are in SRC016.

Conversion: four 4 mm water-cooled off-axis Cu channels after a 5 mm
constriction (4eff), with narrow 1.5 mm reverse-vortex inlets (NGI), give
at 900 mbar the conversion the standard torch reaches at 200 mbar:
~34.5% at 10 ls/min, 2.7 kW without a pump (atmospheric); 55% at 5 ls/min,
2.7 kW; up to ~57% at SEI ~10 eV. Pump removal does not change
conversion. Quenching helps only at low flow (no gain above 20 ls/min).
CO output limited to ~3.5 ls/min by the 3 kW generator.

Energy (absorbed power, reviewer recalculation, 24.055 L/mol):
- eta = X x 2.9/SEI: 24.8% (OP001), 19.7% (OP002), 16.3% (OP003).
- EC_CO2 1130-1722 kJ/mol; EC_CO 14.0-21.3 kWh/Nm3 (absorbed, not wall).
- Reported efficiencies in Figs. 2, 6, 7, 10 and the conclusion (incl.
  "30% at 12%" and "57% at 20%") are ~9-10% above X x dH/SEI with the
  stated 20 C flows; flows taken at 0 C reproduce them. Figs. 8-9 and
  SRC016 (27% at 12.3%) are consistent with the lower values. Use the
  recalculated values. Human check advised.

Duration: not reported (power scans only).

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence
(calibrated MS, expansion-corrected, reproduced in SRC016); energy results
moderate confidence (absorbed power only; efficiency basis inconsistent).
