Hecimovic et al. (2024) - Microwave CO2 plasma vs LT and HT (SOEC) electrolysis (benchmark)
Source ID: SRC026. J. CO2 Util. 83, 102825. Published version (KIT repository).
Comparison paper (IPP, HZB, FZJ, KIT, Stuttgart); no vendor author, no competing interests.
Plasma cooled-channel values duplicate SRC016/SRC017 and are not counted again.

SOEC (benchmark values the review needs):
- FZJ 70 kW system (calculation from measured 800 C short-stack IV, 1.0 A/cm2):
  utilisation 86% (capped to avoid carbon), 17.5 Nm3/h CO, stack 69.6 kW.
  Value used: 3.98 kWh/Nm3 (stack) to 4.19 kWh/Nm3 (with the assumed 0.95
  rectifier), i.e. 88-84%. Reported EPC 3.89 is probably a 3.98 typo. Cell
  ~1.66 V, above thermoneutral, so no external heat for the stack.
- Topsoe eCOs (vendor): utilisation >95%, 99.0 vol% CO standard (up to
  99.999%), 6-200 Nm3/h, EPC_tot 6-8 kWh/Nm3 (website; boundary unknown).
  The 90% wall-plug efficiency in the same column contradicts 6-8 (authors'
  own footnote): 6-8 kWh/Nm3 is 44-58%.
- CO and O2 leave separately; no separation needed beyond CO/CO2.

Plasma (new data only):
- O2-membrane demonstrator (nozzle torch, 21 LCCF membranes at ~1000 C):
  X up to 26%, EPC_tot 35 kWh/Nm3, only 5% of the O2 removed. No efficient
  continuous route to <1% O2 exists yet (authors).
- KEROGREEN 915 MHz, 6 kW, up to 60 slm, TRL 5: X 14%, EPC_process 11.4
  kWh/Nm3; no wall-plug value; website data only.

Other: PSA CO/CO2 separation ~0.25 kWh/Nm3 (cited, Jouny 2018). Magnetron
lifetime 8000 h; solid-state supplies several years. SOEC cold start 4-6 h
with hot standby; plasma minutes. Run-times: SOEC 8000 h, LT 4000 h,
plasma 29.3 h.

Reporting quality (R1-R4): mixed data types (design calculation, vendor,
lab); EPC_total defined but boundaries differ; authors independent of the
vendor. Conversion/product results moderate confidence; energy results
lower confidence for the SOEC values (internal inconsistencies above).
