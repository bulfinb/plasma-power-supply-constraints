Kiefer et al. (2024) - 2.45 GHz microwave plasma torch, CO2 splitting, ~3 kW, with wall-plug metering
Source ID: SRC016. Chem. Eng. J. 481, 148326. Published version (Augsburg repository).
Experimental results only. Pure CO2, no catalyst, no diluent.

Scale: P_abs 0.6-2.7 kW (forward minus reflected microwave power); CO2
3.7-18.6 slm (0 degC). Wall-plug active power of the microwave supply
and vacuum pump measured with 3-phase energy meters (error 1.4%):
3.3-4.2 kW at the selected points. Chiller excluded. No external heat.

Conversion: up to 56.6% (cooling channels + reverse vortex, 3.7 slm,
2.7 kW, 900 mbar). At the best wall-plug point, 34.4% at atmospheric
pressure with no pump (9.3 slm, 2.7 kW). Standard torch near 1 bar
stays below 20%; quenching (cooling channels, nozzle) or 150 mbar
operation is needed for higher conversion. SRC016-E015, E018, E032.

Energy (22.414 L/mol, the authors' basis):
- Best wall-plug efficiency eta_tot 17.9% = EPC 19.6 kWh/Nm3 CO,
  reached both at atmospheric pressure (cooling channels, X 34.4%) and
  at 150 mbar (standard, X 38.6%, pump power included). Reproduced.
- Same points on absorbed power: eta_p 24.9-27.9%; EC_CO2 1011-1135
  kJ/mol (absorbed) vs 1579 kJ/mol (wall).
- Losses at the best point: plasma ~14, magnetron source ~5.7,
  reflected ~0 kWh/Nm3 CO. Magnetron efficiency 72% at 2.7 kW, ~53% at
  0.9 kW, so wall-plug efficiency rises with power while eta_p falls.
- Highest conversion costs energy: OP003 eta_p 16.4%, eta_tot ~10.5%
  (figure), EPC ~33 kWh/Nm3.
- Product is an unseparated CO2/CO/O2 mixture.

Duration: 29 h 20 min durability test in four blocks (cooling channels,
atmospheric, 10.2 slm, 2.25 kW): X ~27%, eta_p ~26%, eta_tot ~17-18%,
stable; 2-3 min start-up. SRC016-E024, E025.

Impurities: N2 up to ~5% helps (eta_p 33.2%); 2% humid air cuts X from
~24% to 10.3% in the nozzle torch; authors call for <=100 ppm H2O.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence (MS
method and expansion handling only referenced, no balance); energy
results higher confidence (absorbed and wall-plug power both measured,
EPC reproduced).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf PDF pp. 2-14 (printed = PDF - 1), Figs.
5-15, 17-19; SRC016-E001-E032. Calculations SRC016-C001-C006,
E041-E046; outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
