Renninger et al. (2022) - DC glow-to-arc transition discharge, magnetically rotated, ~100-220 W, 1 bar
Source ID: SRC022. J. CO2 Util. 58, 101919. Published version. Univ. Stuttgart.
Experimental. CO2 (purity NR), ZrO2 packed bed in the ring electrode.

Scale: smallest in the set: 0.65-1.25 SLM (0.03-0.06 mol/min CO2), plasma
power ~100-220 W. Plasma power = V x I at the reactor; "system" power =
DC output of a lab supply feeding a ZVS driver (61-65% efficient) - NOT
wall plug (lab supply mains losses excluded).

Conversion: 16-29%, set mainly by plasma power; plateau ~29%. Best at
1.25 SLM: 27.4%. Without the ZrO2 bed only 7.5%; upward flow 10.5%.

Energy (recalculated, 0 C standard, dH 12.6 J/SCC):
- OP001 (1.25 SLM, ~162 W plasma, ~262 W supply): eta_plasma 44%,
  eta_system 27.5%; 7.9 kWh/Nm3 CO (plasma), 12.8 (driver input).
- OP002 (~99 W plasma): 17% at eta_plasma 45%, system 29%.
- Best plasma-basis conversion+efficiency pair at 1 bar in the set so far,
  but at ~160 W and with a packed bed of unknown role.
- Text's "45% at 216 W supply" is the 21% point; the conclusion's 27.4% /
  45% / 28% is at ~262 W supply.

Duration: <10 h total; no electrode wear seen. Steady state after >=1 min.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence (NDIR
with GC confirmation; no repeats); energy results moderate confidence (both
boundaries measured, but not wall plug; small scale).
