Girard-Sahun et al. (2022) - Gliding arc plasmatron with a post-plasma charcoal bed, 0.53 kW, 1 bar
Source ID: SRC019. Chem. Eng. J. 442, 136268. Published version + ESI.
Experimental (plus a 0D gasification model, not used as evidence). Pure CO2.

Scale: plasma power 0.533 kW (V x I; AC current-source supply), 10 Ln/min
CO2, SEI 3.2 kJ/L. ~5 g charcoal bed at the anode exit, silo-fed. No
supply efficiency. Carbon is a co-reactant.

Conversion and products:
- No bed: X 7.6%, CO 7.2%, O2 3.7%, steady.
- With bed, maximum (first ~1 min): X 12.6%, CO 21.9%, O2 0.2%.
- With bed after ~3.5 min the gain is gone; at 14 min X ~4.7% (below the
  no-bed value) as oxygen complexes saturate the carbon; the silo does not
  restore it. O2 stays below 0.8% throughout (O2 removal persists), but
  by 14 min CO (7.3%) and energy per CO are back to the no-bed level.

Energy (plasma power):
- Reported: eta 27.9% -> 45.4%, EC 41.9 -> 25.4 kJ/L. Calculated with
  283 kJ/mol per CO2 converted (pure splitting) despite carbon consumption.
- Reviewer O/C balance at the maximum: 0.121 mol C consumed and 0.246 mol CO
  per mol CO2 fed = net CO2 + C -> 2 CO; net, carbon supplies half the CO
  (a balance result; bed carbon may partly leave as CO2).
  With the carbon counted as an energy input, eta_LHV = 55% at the
  maximum (no bed 27%), ~24% at 14 min.
- Electricity per CO: 13.0 (no bed), 3.95 (bed max), 12.8 (bed, 14 min)
  kWh/Nm3 CO, plus 0.21-0.26 kg carbon per Nm3 CO (LHV ~2-2.4 kWh/Nm3).
- SEI converted with 24.5 L/mol on Ln/min flows: values ~9% different if
  Ln is 0 C.

Duration: 14 min runs, triplicates; time-resolved data show the decay.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence
(calibrated NDIR/O2/GC, expansion-corrected, triplicates, controls); energy
results lower confidence as reported (pure-splitting enthalpy credited,
transient maximum), moderate for the recalculated values.

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 3-14, Figs. 3-7; ESI Figs. S8-S9;
SRC019-E001-E018. Calculations SRC019-C001-C005, E041-E044;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md, outputs/evidence.csv.
