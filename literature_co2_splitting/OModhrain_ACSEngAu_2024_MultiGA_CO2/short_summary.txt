O'Modhrain et al. (2024) - Multi-reactor gliding arc plasmatron (5 x ~1 kW), plug-power basis, 1 bar
Source ID: SRC020. ACS Eng. Au 4, 333-344. Published version + SI.
Experimental. CO2 99.7%. Prototype built by D-CRBN (spin-off); PSUs from a D-CRBN-affiliated
company; no competing interest declared.

Scale: five reverse-vortex GAP nodes in one housing, one DC switching PSU
(~1 kW, current-regulated, no ballast) per node; 30-80 L/min; up to 7.1 kW
PLUG power (three-phase meter). PSU plug-to-plasma efficiency measured at
77-83%. Auxiliaries (chiller, PLC, fan) on the meter: not stated.

Conversion: low. 8.7% at 80 L/min with five nodes (best); ~7.6% with three;
~2.1% with one. Conversion peaks at ~5.3 kJ/L plug SEI; parallel nodes add
throughput (188 -> 779 g/h CO2 converted), not conversion. Hot post-plasma
chamber (~600 C) limits conversion at low flow.

Energy (plug power, 23.6 L/mol, recalculated):
- OP001 (5 nodes): eta 19.6%, EC 1.44 MJ/mol = 17.9 kWh/Nm3 CO; plasma
  basis (PSU 0.77-0.83) 23.6-25.4%, 13.8-14.9 kWh/Nm3.
- Plug efficiency 20-23% across configurations; comparable wall-plug energy
  per CO to the microwave torch of SRC016 (19.6 kWh/Nm3) but at a quarter
  of its conversion (8.7% vs 34%).
- Text gives the 1-node optimum as 23% at 1.4 kJ/L with 187 g/h; these do
  not agree (17.9%); Fig. 3a power gives 1.11 kJ/L and 22.7% (used).

Costs (author claims, one line): assembly ~3000 EUR/kW incl. PSU; PSU ~400
EUR/kW (target 150, >90% efficient); separation excluded.

Duration: not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(calibrated O2/CO2 sensors, triplicates; CO not measured); energy results
higher confidence (plug power metered, PSU efficiency measured).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 2-10, Figs. 3-5, Table 1; ESI pp. s4-s9;
SRC020-E001-E017. Calculations SRC020-C001-C003, E041-E043;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md, outputs/evidence.csv.
