Bryssinck et al. (2025) - 7-node gliding arc pilot (D-CRBN) with carbon bed and recirculation, ~8.5 kW plug
Source ID: SRC021. React. Chem. Eng. 10, 1910-1923. Accepted author version (UAntwerp) + ESI.
Experimental (plus a 2D post-plasma model, not used as evidence). CO2 99.7%.
Pilot developed by D-CRBN; two authors at D-CRBN; no conflicts declared.

Scale: seven ~1 kW DC switching supplies; 7.6-9.5 kW PLUG power ("total
power of the system", in a 10 ft container); 50-110 L/min; 50 g activated
carbon bed 35-65 mm after the nodes; silo feed; 50% recycle option.

Conversion and products (bed at 35 mm):
- Best: 50 L/min, semi-continuous feed: X 18-20%, CO up to 33 vol%, O2 <1%.
- 70 L/min: X ~16%, CO 27%; at 65 mm only 8% (cooler bed).
- Recycle 50%: X 10 -> 13% of total inlet, but CO output fell.
- Runs of minutes only.

Energy (plug power):
- Reported EC 5.8 MWh/t CO (best) = 7.3 kWh/Nm3 CO (plug), plus 0.27 kg
  carbon per Nm3 CO (~300 g C/h). Authors' plasma-basis 4.8 MWh/t.
- Net reaction with the bed is CO2 + C -> 2CO: the reported EC values equal
  two CO per CO2 converted. With the carbon counted as an energy input,
  eta_LHV = 36% at the plug (41% plasma basis; 24% as 283 x X / SEI).
- Recycle: EC 10.9 -> 13.7 MWh/t (+1 kW compressor).
- Benchmark (no bed) is inconsistent: 12% CO needs ~12.8% conversion
  without carbon; the reported 6.4% and 13.5 MWh/t both imply CO = 2X, as
  if O2 were taken as zero. The claimed "6% -> 20%" gain is probably
  ~13% -> 20%. Human check advised.

Duration: ~200-560 s runs; no long-term test.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence with the
bed, lower for the benchmark; energy results moderate confidence (plug
basis, reproducible arithmetic; carbon not counted; metered scope unclear).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf (author version) pp. 3-30, Figs. 5-12;
SRC021-E001-E018. Calculations SRC021-C001-C006, E041-E044;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md, outputs/evidence.csv.
