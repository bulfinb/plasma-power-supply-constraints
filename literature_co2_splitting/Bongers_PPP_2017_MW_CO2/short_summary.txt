Bongers et al. (2017) - 915 MHz (up to 10 kW) and 2.45 GHz (1 kW) microwave CO2 plasmas, 200-600 mbar
Source ID: SRC018. Plasma Process. Polym. 14, e1600126. Wiley Early View typeset version
(cite by DOI). DIFFER / IGVP Stuttgart. Experimental. Pure CO2.

Scale: 915 MHz system rated 3-30 kW, run at 3.1-10 kW, 11-75 slm, 200 mbar,
nozzle quench with supersonic exhaust. 2.45 GHz system 1 kW. Power is "MW
power" (forward/absorbed not defined). Sub-atmospheric: vacuum pumping
excluded. No wall-plug data. No external heat.

Conversion: highest single-pass conversion in the set so far: 83% at
11 slm, 8.1 kW (SEI 10.3 eV), 200 mbar. 47% at 3.1 kW. At 75 slm, 10-23%.

Energy (MW power basis, 22.414 L/mol; recalculated = alpha x 2.9/SEI):
- 11 slm: eta 24% at 83% conversion (EC_CO 14.8 kWh/Nm3) to 35% at 47%
  (10.0 kWh/Nm3).
- 75 slm: eta 51% at ~10% (6.9 kWh/Nm3, lowest energy per CO so far) to
  ~36% at ~22.5% (9.9 kWh/Nm3). Text gives 40% at 10 kW; Fig. 3b and the
  paper's own alpha/SEI give ~36%.
- 2.45 GHz reverse vortex: ~46% at ~12.5% conversion, 400-600 mbar.
- Supersonic expansion at 1 mbar: at most 15%.

Mechanism: OES Tg 4000-5700 K, Tvib 9000 K; dissociation mainly thermal;
results near the thermal limit (~50% with ideal quenching). Early high
efficiencies (Fridman, below 1 eV) not reproduced. Authors suggest
coupling SOECs to microwave plasma to lift the conversion limit.

Duration: not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence (MS
against calibrated mixtures; expansion handling and balance not given);
energy results lower confidence (power boundary undefined; pump excluded at
200 mbar; one text value conflicts with the figure).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 1-7, Figs. 3-9, Table 1; SRC018-E001-E017.
