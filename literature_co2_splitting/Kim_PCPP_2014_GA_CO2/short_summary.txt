Kim, Lim & Chun (2014) - 3-phase AC gliding arc plasmatron, 0.6 kW, pure CO2 6-32 L/min
Source ID: SRC028. Plasma Chem. Plasma Process. 34, 125-143. Published version.
Chosun University. Experimental. Pure CO2 (Case 1); CH4/H2O co-feed cases out of scope.

Scale: one plasmatron, three blade electrodes, 0.6 kW "plasma input power"
at every point (boundary and method not stated; likely a setpoint). Supply
frequency not stated. No wall-plug data. External heat: feed preheated in
a 400 C steam generator (Fig. 1, BB reading; not stated in the text), not
counted below. Pressure not stated (~1 atm assumed).

Conversion: reported CR up to 12.3% at 14 L/min. CR is the drop in CO2
concentration, without gas-expansion correction; the paper's own CO
selectivity is only ~57%. Carbon-based conversion from the measured CO:
7.8% at 14 L/min, 6.4% at 20 L/min.

Energy (plasma input power, 22.414 L/mol, carbon-based X):
- 14 L/min: SEI 0.60 eV; eta 38%; EC_CO 9.1 kWh/Nm3.
- 20 L/min: SEI 0.42 eV; eta 45%; EC_CO 7.8 kWh/Nm3 (best).
- On the reported CR these would be 60% and 72%: the source of the
  ~61-65% gliding-arc efficiency quoted for this paper in reviews.
- If the feed was preheated to 400 C: ~30-32% (indicative, C005).

Duration: not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results lower confidence
(concentration-based conversion, no balance, ~2-3% unidentified gas, O2/CO
~0.4); energy results lower confidence (single fixed power of unstated
boundary; flow reference not stated, +9% at 25 C).
