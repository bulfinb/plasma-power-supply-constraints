Van Laer & Bogaerts (2015) - ZrO2 packed-bed DBD, ~20-37 W plasma, 1 bar (DBD class representative)
Source ID: SRC024. Energy Technol. 3, 1038-1044. Published version (UAntwerp repository).
Experimental (plus 2D He fluid model, not used as evidence). Pure CO2.

Scale: coaxial DBD, 15.9 cm3 discharge volume, 20-100 mL/min, "applied
power" 60-100 W (meaning not defined); plasma power (V x I) used for
efficiency but not reported per point; backed out at ~20-37 W, i.e. 33-46%
of the applied power.

Conversion: up to 42% (2.0-2.24 mm beads, 20 mL/min, 80 W); best combination
37.8% at 6.4% (1.6-1.8 mm, 60 W, 20 mL/min), ~1.9x the empty reactor. Taken
from the drop in GC CO2 signal without stated expansion correction; corrected
values would be ~29% and ~33%.

Energy (plasma power): efficiency 4.7-9.6%; 36-74 kWh/Nm3 CO (98 for the
empty reactor), 3-5x the microwave and gliding-arc results; ~2-3% on applied
power. DBD conversion is high only at very high specific energy.

Duration: steady state after 20 min; no long test.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate-to-lower confidence
(likely uncorrected for expansion); energy results lower confidence (plasma
power not reported, molar volume not stated, applied power undefined).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 1-5, Eqs. (1)-(4); SRC024-E001-E013.
