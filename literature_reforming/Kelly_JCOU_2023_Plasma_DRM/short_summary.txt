Kelly et al. (2023) - dry reforming of methane in a 1 kW, 2.45 GHz atmospheric-pressure microwave plasma
Source ID: SRC011. J. CO2 Util. 75, 102564 (open access). Experimental results only.
No catalyst. No ESI located.

Scale: Plasma scale 1 kW absorbed microwave power (six-port reflectometer;
reflected < 1%). 10 nL/min CO2+CH4 (CH4 0.13-0.19 mol/min); SEI 6 kJ/L.
No external heating, so absorbed power is the whole process input apart
from the (unreported) wall-plug efficiency of the solid-state source.

Conversion: CO2 46-49% and CH4 55-67% (total weighted 50-54%) at 30-45%
CH4, corrected for gas expansion with a measured probe-gas factor. CO and
H2 are the main products (H2/CO 0.56 to 1.01); water 7-14% (O balance);
C2H2 0.7-1.6%. Carbon formation limits CH4 to 45% (50/50 extinguishes);
the carbon is amorphous carbon black, 21 nm primary particles.

Energy (power basis = absorbed microwave power; no external heat):
- EC_CH4 581-723 kJ/mol CH4 converted; per mol CH4+CO2 converted 270-291
  kJ/mol (2.8-3.0 eV), reproducing the authors' energy cost.
- EC_H2 330-522 kJ/mol; EY_H2 14-22 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV ~66-68% (end points).
- Author metric: energy cost = SEI / total conversion, 2.8-3.0 eV/molecule
  (11.1-11.9 kJ/L), below the 4.27 eV competitiveness target; syngas
  energy cost 1.7-1.9 eV.
- eta_HHV (partial; source losses excluded) 45-53%.

Duration: steady state after ~15 min; total time on stream not reported.

System information: solid-state 2.45 GHz source, 16 mm quartz tube, swirl
flow, electrode-free. Exhaust ~680-730 K at 15 cm. Solid carbon, C2H4/C2H6
and wall-plug power not reported. Table 1 identifies Chun et al. (2017, 6
kW) and Sun et al. (2021, 2 kW) as further MW DRM candidates.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence (calibrated
analysers, measured gas expansion, stated uncertainties, atom balances);
energy results higher confidence (absorbed power measured, no external
heat; wall-plug boundary missing).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 3-9, Table 1, Figs. 1-6; SRC011-E001-E022.
Calculations SRC011-C001-C002, E041-E045; outputs/calculations.py. Details:
outputs/extraction.md and outputs/evidence.csv.
