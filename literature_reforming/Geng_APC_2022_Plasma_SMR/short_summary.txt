Geng et al. (2022) - nonthermal DBD plasma-assisted catalytic SMR
SRC002. Experimental results only. Thermal background retained.

Scale: 10-20 W reported discharge power, usually 15 W. Not total system
electricity. Methane feed about 1.0e-3 mol/min (about 25 mL/min), derived
from the water feed at S/C 4.4 (SRC002-C003; reviewer interpretation of
the unclear feed wording, needs human confirmation).
Thermal background: 300-440 degC; reactor heating and steam-generation
duties not reported. Plasma-off controls remain heated.

Conversion: Ni catalyst gives about 7% with plasma off versus 60% at 15 W,
both at 300 degC and S/C 4.4. Nearly 80% at 15 W and 440 degC; about 74%
at 20 W and 300 degC in a separate power sweep. Do not combine 20 W with
the 80% result.

Energy (power basis; furnace and steam heat excluded, not reported):
- EC_CH4 about 1490 kJ/mol (15.5 eV/molecule) at 300 degC, 15 W, and
  about 1120 kJ/mol (11.6 eV/molecule) at 440 degC, 15 W. Both are far
  above the 206 kJ/mol SMR reaction enthalpy.
- EC_H2 about 450-490 kJ/mol H2 and EY_H2 about 15-16 g H2/kWh at
  300 degC; about 340-370 kJ/mol and 20-22 g/kWh at 440 degC.
- No author energy efficiency (88-90% H2 selectivity is not one).
  eta_P,LHV and eta_HHV not calculable.

Duration: About 2 h demonstration; Ni time series reaches about 130 min
after plasma ignition. A separate stepped-power series reaches about
225 min with changing settings; not a single-condition endurance test.
No long-term durability demonstration.

System information: Boundary is the DBD reactor; heater type and duty,
steam generator duty, supply efficiency and wall power not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(strong matched controls; unclear feed wording and carbon-balance
definition); energy results lower confidence (discharge power only, not
reconstructable; unquantified furnace heat).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 2-6, Figs. 2-6; SRC002-E003, E005-E007,
E017, E020-E022, E026-E028, E033-E048, E054-E055, E058, E061. Energy
metrics: SRC002-C003-C005, E065-E070. Detailed records, calculations and
reporting quality: outputs/extraction.md and outputs/evidence.csv.
