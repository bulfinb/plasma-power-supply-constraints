Wanten et al. (2022) - dry reforming in a confined atmospheric pressure glow discharge, ~0.1 kW
Source ID: SRC015. J. CO2 Util. 56, 101869; ESI sections 1-3.
Experimental results only (kinetic model excluded). No catalyst.

Scale: Plasma scale 97-145 W (SEI x flow; SEI 3.4-12.5 kJ/L read from
Fig. 4), 20-35 mA, 0.5-2 L/min CO2/CH4 (15-35% CH4), no diluent.
Single glow in a 5 mm ceramic tube. HV DC supply with a 300 kOhm series
ballast resistor; wall power not reported.

Conversion: confining the glow pushes most gas through the plasma: at
25% CH4, 25 mA, 0.5 L/min X_CH4 94.3%, X_CO2 63.7% (X_tot 71%); best
X_tot 74% at 35% CH4. CO-rich syngas (H2/CO 0.31-0.63) with much of the
hydrogen as water (H-based S_H2O 26-71%); C-based S_CO 95-100%, C2 <4%.
CH4 >35% gives carbon and unstable plasma; higher power damaged the
tube, cathode and resistor.

Energy (power basis = plasma power; no external heat; 24.05 L/mol):
- EC_CH4 813-1194 kJ/mol CH4 converted; 3.38-4.24 eV per CH4+CO2
  converted (author 3.5-4.2 eV, reproduced). ~7% lower at 0 degC litres.
- EC_H2 560-1339 kJ/mol; EY_H2 5.4-13.0 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV 51.6-62.3%.
- Author EC (13.9-17 kJ/L, per CH4+CO2 converted) looks low partly
  because feeds are 65-85% CO2; EC_CH4 is the comparable metric.
- Ballast resistor: I^2R 188-368 W, 1.6-2.6x the plasma power, so the
  plasma receives only 28-39% of the supply output; EC_CH4 at supply
  output 2392-3490 kJ/mol. A lab stabilisation method, not intrinsic,
  but every parallel glow needs current limiting at scale.
- eta_HHV (partial; ballast and supply losses excluded) 31.7-53.4%.

Duration: blank + three plasma measurements per condition; run
duration and time on stream not reported.

System information: lab single-glow reactor, Macor tube, 310S
electrodes. No temperature measurements (model assumes 2000-3000 K).
Wall power, supply efficiency, heat losses, costs NR.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence
(N2 internal standard, calibrated GC, triplicates, headline values in
text); energy results moderate confidence (plasma-power method not
described, SEI figure-read, litre reference implied by 24.05 L/mol).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 1-9, Figs. 3-7; ESI.pdf pp. 1-4, 7-8,
12-13; SRC015-E001-E027. Calculations SRC015-C001-C006, E041-E046;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
