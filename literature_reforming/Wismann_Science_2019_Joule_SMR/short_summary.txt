Wismann et al. (2019) - Joule-heated steam methane reforming
Source ID: SRC001. Experimental results only; industrial modelling excluded.
Role: lab-scale Joule-heating benchmark; From 2024 (SRC005) is the primary, pilot-scale one.

Scale: Electrical input power not reported. Total wet feed of 340-1700
Nml/min, containing 30 mol% CH4, corresponds to methane input of 102-510
Nml/min. Normal-flow reference temperature and pressure unspecified.

Conversion: Labelled experimental conditions span 50.9-92.7% CH4 conversion.
Highest: 92.7% at 102 Nml/min CH4 input (340 Nml/min total feed). At the
largest feed, 510 Nml/min CH4, the highest labelled conversion is 85.0%.
These are different operating points.

Energy: EC_CH4 and the other power-basis metrics cannot be calculated
because no point-matched electrical power or product flows are reported.
No author energy efficiency. eta_HHV not calculable. Compare with plasma
sources on conversion and conditions only.

Duration: At least four consecutive GC measurements per condition, GC
every 15 min; model fitting describes averaging over at least 45 min.
Total time on stream not reported. No discernible activity loss between
initial and final measurements; elapsed time unspecified.

System information: Lab boundary is the insulated resistance-heated tube;
power supply, preheat and steam generation duties not reported. Industrial
volume comparison with a side-fired reformer (SRC001-OP013) is a design
projection and excludes power supply and furnace auxiliaries.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence; energy
results lower confidence (no electrical power or thermal duties reported).
