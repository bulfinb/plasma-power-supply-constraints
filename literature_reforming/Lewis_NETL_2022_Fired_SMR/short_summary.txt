NETL (2022) - fired steam methane reforming reference plant (Case 1)
Source ID: SRC003. Role: fired SMR benchmark. Data type: Aspen Plus design
model of a commercial single-train plant; not measured plant data.
DOE/NETL-2022/3241. Only Case 1 (SMR without CO2 capture) extracted.

Scale: 20,126 kg/h H2 (222 kNm3/h), 99.90 vol% at 6.48 MPa. Natural gas
1,031 MW HHV (feed plus fuel). Grid electricity 12.65 MWe for auxiliaries,
79% of it for H2 compression. No electrical reactor heating.

Conversion: primary reformer 77.7% CH4 conversion (S/C 2.56, 871 degC
outlet, 2.8 MPa); 76.6% at plant level after shift.

Energy (heat basis, reformer level; our calculations from stream table):
- Process-gas heat uptake Q_abs 264 MW; burner fuel Q_fired 448 MW HHV
  (397 MW LHV), mostly recycled PSA off-gas.
- EC_CH4,abs 340 kJ/mol and EC_CH4,fired 577 kJ/mol HHV (510 LHV), per
  mol CH4 converted in the reformer; reaction enthalpy 206 kJ/mol.
- Author: HHV effective thermal efficiency 75.4%, cold gas 76.7%
  (LHV 70.5% / 71.9%). Ours: eta_HHV 75.7%, f_electric 1.2%; export steam
  (about 143 MW) excluded and not credited.
- 184 MJ NG (HHV) and 0.63 kWh electricity per kg H2.

Duration: not applicable (design basis; 90% capacity factor assumed).

System information: full plant boundary with stream table, closed carbon
balance and energy balance (1.3 GJ/h unaccounted). Reformer duties, tube
count and reformer volume not stated. Stack 9.3 kg CO2/kg H2; LCOH
$1.06/kg (Dec 2018 USD, 73% fuel).

Reporting quality (R1-R4): design basis; plant boundary and HHV/LHV basis
stated; independent government source, peer reviewed. Confidence higher
for plant-level values, moderate for our derived reformer duties.

Traceability: PDF pp. 69-95 (printed 51-77), Exhibits 3-1 to 3-16;
SRC003-E001-E040. Calculations SRC003-C001-C006, E041-E050. Details:
