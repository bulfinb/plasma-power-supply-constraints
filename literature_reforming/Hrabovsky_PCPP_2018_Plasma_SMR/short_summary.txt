Hrabovsky et al. (2018) - steam and dry methane reforming in a ~100 kW water/argon-stabilized DC arc plasma
Source ID: SRC007. Plasma Chem. Plasma Process. 38, 743-758. Experimental results only;
equilibrium calculations excluded. No catalyst. No ESI located.

Scale: Plasma scale 88-136 kW arc power (torch electrical input); 120 kW
in the steam reforming run, 89-116 kW in the dry reforming run. Plasma jet
power is 57-60% of arc power (torch efficiency). CH4 75-150 slm (3.3-6.7
mol/min) into a 200 L reactor. No external furnace or steam generator:
water is fed liquid and evaporated by the plasma, so arc power is the
total process heat. Supply losses and auxiliaries not reported.

Conversion (our calculations from Figs. 7-8, argon tracer): SMR 91.3% at
150 slm CH4 to 99.1% at 75 slm, all at 120 kW; DRM 83-95% CH4 and 89-92%
CO2 at 89-116 kW. Author maxima 99.5% CH4, CO2 at most 93%. H2/CO 2.9-3.3
(SMR) and 1.1-1.25 (DRM). S/C about 1. Composition near equilibrium.

Energy (power basis = arc power; no external heat to add):
- EC_CH4 1179 kJ/mol (SMR, 150 slm) to 2172 (75 slm); DRM 1149-1309.
  About 5-10 times the reaction enthalpy (206 SMR, 247 DRM); hot product
  gas (1200-1400 K) is water-quenched without heat recovery.
- EC_H2 198-369 MJ/kg (SMR), ~297 MJ/kg (DRM); EY_H2 9.8-18.2 g/kWh;
  eta_P,LHV 33-49%.
- Author metric: H2 energy cost on "process enthalpy" basis (arc power
  minus torch and reactor losses): minimum 79 MJ/kg (steam; 86 in the
  Results text) and 116 MJ/kg (dry). On arc power: minimum 192 MJ/kg
  (steam); our OP001 gives 198 MJ/kg on the same basis.
- eta_HHV (partial: excludes supply losses and auxiliaries) 36-51%.

Duration: steady segments of 2-5 min in two ~17 min test runs; composition
responds within 30 s. Total time on stream and torch electrode life not
reported. Not an endurance test.

System information: torch, 200 L reactor, water quench, filter, scrubber,
water ejector, afterburner. Torch needs cooling water and argon. Reactor
loss per point, mass/carbon balance, solid carbon mass, calibration and
costs not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(matched feeds and power, tracer flow, our readings reproduce the authors'
plotted conversions; all graph-read, no balance closure); energy results
moderate confidence (point-matched arc power; slm reference and per-point
losses not reported).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 7-14 (printed 749-756), Table 1, Figs.
7-16; SRC007-E001-E039. Calculations SRC007-C001-C008, E041-E052;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
