Kwon, Kim & Song (2023) - dry reforming in a rotating gliding arc with product quenching, ~1.7 kW
Source ID: SRC014. J. CO2 Util. 70, 102448 (open access); ESI section S1.
Experimental results only (equilibrium modelling excluded). No catalyst.

Scale: Plasma scale 0.92-1.67 kW discharge power (V x I on oscilloscope;
measured SEI 2.3-4.2 kJ/L). Feed 24 SLPM of which 12 SLPM N2; CH4 3-6
SLPM (0.13-0.27 mol/min). No external heating; supply wall power and
cooling-water duties (electrode and quenching rod) not reported.

Conversion: at CH4/CO2 5/7 and SEI 4.2 kJ/L, quenching lowers conversion
slightly (CH4 ~52 to 51.1%, CO2 ~40.6 to 40.2%) but raises H2 selectivity
from 81 to 85% and cuts water from 13.9 to 8.6% by suppressing reverse
water-gas shift; H2/CO 0.91 to 0.95. At CH4/CO2 = 1 (quenched) H2/CO
1.26, but carbon deposits (C balance 97%). Conversion roughly halves at
SEI 2.3 kJ/L. N2 internal standard; C balance 97-100%.

Energy (power basis = discharge power; no external heat; 0 degC SLPM):
- EC_CH4 740-948 kJ/mol CH4 converted; 4.3-4.7 eV per CH4+CO2 molecule
  converted. ~7% higher if SLPM is at 20 degC.
- EC_H2 425-533 kJ/mol; EY_H2 13.6-17.1 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV 52.7-54.7%.
- Author eta (same definition as eta_P,LHV): 55.3% at 5/7 quenched (55.6%
  elsewhere in the text), 53.2% at 1/1; reproduced within ~0.6 points.
  Author conversion cost 18.5-20.4 kJ/L and syngas cost 10.2-11.9 kJ/L are
  per litre of total flow and include the 50% N2 dilution.
- eta_HHV (partial; cooling and supply losses excluded) 35.5-38.0%.

Duration: ~10 min stabilisation per sample, three repeats; electrodes
need a 3 min CO2 burn-off of carbon between runs. No time-on-stream test.

System information: lab RGA, swirl flow, water-cooled Cu inner electrode,
water-cooled quenching rod. Only off-axis thermocouples (not the gas
temperature). Wall power, cooling duties, pressure, costs NR.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence
(calibrated GC, N2 expansion correction, triplicates, matched quench and
uncooled-rod controls); energy results higher confidence (measured
discharge power per point, no external heat, author efficiency matches
our definition; SLPM reference and supply losses unknown).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 3-12, Table 1, Figs. 2, 6-12; ESI.pdf
pp. 1-2; SRC014-E001-E022. Calculations SRC014-C001-C005, E041-E046;
