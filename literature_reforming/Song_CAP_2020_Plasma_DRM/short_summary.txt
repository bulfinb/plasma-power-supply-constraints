Song et al. (2020) - dry reforming of methane with a 20-30 kW, 400 kHz inductively coupled plasma torch
Source ID: SRC009. Curr. Appl. Phys. 20, 196-204. Experimental results only;
CHEMKIN-PRO simulations excluded. No catalyst. No ESI located.

Scale: Plasma scale 17-27 kW RF power (generator "input/applied" power;
the text calls the plotted value "delivered, not absorbed" while figure
captions say "absorbed"; boundary unclear). CO2 20-30 SLM (plasma gas,
with Ar at ignition) and CH4 20-36 SLM (0.9-1.6 mol/min) injected into the
plasma tail. No external heating; generator wall power not reported.

Conversion: CO2/CH4 up to 94/94% at 27 kW with 30/30 SLM (abstract: "95%
for both"); 98% CH4 at 22 kW with 30/20 SLM. At 22 kW, raising CH4 from 20
to 36 SLM lowers CH4 conversion 98 to 79% and raises C2 hydrocarbons
(C2H2 up to 3.3 SLM). Carbon balance 96-99.9% (reported). Water not
measured. No temperature measured.

Energy (power basis = RF generator power; no external heat):
- EC_CH4 1036-1510 kJ/mol (22 kW points) and 1148-1288 (30/30 SLM,
  21-27 kW); per mol CH4+CO2 converted 541-778 kJ/mol (5.6-8.1 eV).
- EC_H2 606-1143 kJ/mol; EY_H2 6-12 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV (H2+CO) 35-51%.
- Author metric: energy conversion efficiency = LHV of H2, CO and C2
  products / (input power + LHV of CH4 converted); 47-57% at 22 kW, best
  57% at 30/36 SLM CO2/CH4 (CH4 conversion 79% there, not 95%). Our
  recalculation reproduces it to within 0.2 points.
- eta_HHV (partial, H2+CO; generator losses excluded) 27-52%.

Duration: not reported (10 min GC analysis per sample). No stability or
lifetime data.

System information: torch and 80 x 267 mm quartz tube; CO2 throughput
capacity ~1.6 SLM/kW. Product flow method, Ar flow during reforming,
water, temperatures, generator efficiency and cooling not reported.
Co-author/funder EN2CORE Technology Inc.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(carbon balance reported and reproducible; no temperatures, repeats or
water); energy results moderate confidence (point-matched power and
reproducible author efficiency, but power boundary contradictory and wall
power missing).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 2-9 (printed 197-204), Figs. 3-9,
Eqs. (1)-(15); SRC009-E001-E027. Calculations SRC009-C001-C006,
E041-E047; outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
