From et al. (2025) - electrified (Joule-heated) SMR of biogas with feed-forward control, pilot plant
Source ID: SRC006. Role: Joule-heating benchmark, pilot scale; companion to
From 2024 (SRC005, same plant). Experimental results only; thermodynamic
simulations excluded. Topsoe eSMR/eREACT, Aarhus University.

Scale: Biogas 6-6.4 Nm3/h plus steam and co-fed H2; CH4 feed about
2.5-2.7 mol/min (SRC006-C001). Electrical power not reported.

Conversion: Authors report syngas composition, not conversion. At 900 degC,
6 barg, 80 h average, the outlet is at equilibrium (CH4 0.24% wet); our
calculation gives X_CH4 about 98% and X_CO2 about 42-54% (SRC006-C002).
Syngas module 1.99 +/- 0.01 with control vs 1.93-2.06 without. Stable down
to normalized S/HC 1.31 and up to 1050 degC.

Energy: none reported (no power, efficiency or energy consumption; data
stated confidential). EC_CH4 and eta_HHV not calculable. Use From 2024
(SRC005) for eSMR energy data.

Duration: 80 h controlled test; about 1000 h in this work.

System information: same pilot train as SRC005, plus real-time CH4
analyser and PLC control of steam and H2. Added CO2 flow, Table 2 steam and
H2 flows, outlet flows and reactor size not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(equilibrium composition, elemental ratios close within 0.3%; conversion
is ours and inlet split graph-read); energy results not available. Topsoe
co-authors develop the technology; one has a related patent pending.

Traceability: Manuscript.pdf pp. 4-8, Figs. 4-5, Tables 1-2; ESI.docx
S1-S5; SRC006-E001-E021. Calculations SRC006-C001-C004, E041-E044;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
