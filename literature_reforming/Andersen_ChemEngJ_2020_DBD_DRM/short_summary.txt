Andersen et al. (2020) - plasma-catalytic dry reforming in a coaxial packed-bed DBD, 45 W
Source ID: SRC013. Chem. Eng. J. 397, 125519; ESI Tables S.1-S.3, Figs. S.1-S.5.
Experimental results only.

Scale: Plasma scale 45 W plasma power (oscilloscope: HV probe, Rogowski coil,
charge capacitor), 3 kHz, fixed for all runs. Feed 25 + 25 Nml/min CH4/CO2
(CH4 ~1.0 mmol/min), SEI 54 J/ml. No external heating or steam, so plasma
power is the whole process input apart from unreported amplifier losses.
Non-thermal DBD; discharge gap packed with ~11 g catalyst beads.

Conversion: plasma only 33% CH4, 22% CO2. Packing does not help: Al2O3 and
Ag/Al2O3 lower CH4 to 27%; Pt matches plasma only; Cu lowers CO2 to 16%.
Catalyst-only (plasma off) ~0%. H2/CO 0.84-1.04. Ethane ~11%, propane ~4%,
methanol up to 3.2% (Cu). C/H/O balances 83-92%; 39-48% of converted
carbon is unidentified. No synergy between plasma and catalysts.

Energy (power basis = plasma power; no external heat):
- EC_CH4 8100-9700 kJ/mol CH4 converted (~33x the DRM enthalpy); 50-56 eV
  per CH4+CO2 molecule converted. ~9% lower if Nml is at 0 degC.
- EC_H2 13,000-16,000 kJ/mol; EY_H2 ~0.5 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV 2.9-3.8%.
- Author metrics: E = 0.183-0.206 mmol/kJ (CH4+CO2 converted per kJ) and
  eta = 3.4-4.4% (DRM + ethane enthalpy / SEI); reproduced (C001). Authors
  state only ~4% of the plasma power goes into conversion.
- eta_HHV (partial; amplifier losses excluded) 2.6-3.4%.

Duration: 5 h cumulative per packing as 5 x 1 h plasma on/off cycles, 1 h
stabilisation before each sample; plasma only 1 h. CH4 conversion steady.

System information: lab coaxial DBD, 17.7 cm3 discharge volume. Carbon
deposits on metal-containing packings; inner-electrode metal (Fe, Cr, Ni)
migrates onto the beads. Gas/bed temperature, applied voltage, wall power
and costs not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(internal standard, triplicates, plasma-only and catalyst-only controls,
but 39-48% of converted carbon unidentified); energy results higher
confidence (plasma power measured continuously, no external heat, author
metrics reproducible; Nml reference and supply losses unknown).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 2-7, Tables 1, 3, 4, Fig. 1; ESI.pdf pp.
4-6, Table S.2, Fig. S.2; SRC013-E001-E026. Calculations SRC013-C001-C005,
