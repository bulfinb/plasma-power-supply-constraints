From et al. (2024) - electrified (Joule-heated) SMR of biogas, pilot plant
Source ID: SRC005. Role: Joule-heating benchmark at pilot scale (primary;
Wismann 2019, SRC001, is the lab-scale complement). Experimental results
only; scale-up modelling excluded. Topsoe eSMR/eREACT, Aarhus University.

Scale: 15-27 Nm3/h in the energy tests, read as total process gas (biogas
+ steam; the text says biogas), about 2.7-6.0 mol/min CH4 (CH4 content
50-75%, point values NR; SRC005-C006, BB decision 2026-11-27). Electrical
power not tabulated; the Fig. 5 capacity axis (about 0.024-0.035 MW) is
read as the power-supply input Q_in, i.e. about 23-35 kW (C005, C006).

Conversion: 97% CH4 at 900 degC, 10 barg, S/C 2.7, at equilibrium. 94% at
950 degC, 20 barg, S/C 2.1, unchanged between 485 h and 1535 h. Feed
contains CO2, so the chemistry is mixed steam/CO2 reforming.

Energy: Author reactor efficiency 72-80% (Q_reaction/Q_in, includes
heating gas 400 to 900 degC; rest is heat loss) at 900 degC, 10 barg.
EC_CH4 and other power-basis metrics not calculable (no point-matched
power, feed composition or conversion). eta_HHV not calculable (preheat,
steam, compression and supply losses not reported). Modelled 99% and
1.0 kWh/Nm3 at >=1 MW excluded.
Reviewer estimate (2026-11-20): EC_CH4 about 390-485 kJ/mol at Q_in,
from equilibrium Q_reaction (308-347 kJ/mol) / author efficiency
(C004); pilot about 23-35 kW if Fig. 5 capacity is Q_in (C005).

Duration: close to 2000 h total; matched stability points 1050 h apart.
Start-up from idle to 900 degC in 2.6 h; heating up to 330 degC/h.

System information: pilot train described (sulfur guards, compressor,
electric boiler, preheat, prereformer, eSMR, waste heat boiler), but
auxiliary duties, reactor size and balance closure not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence (GC,
1050 h matched comparison; feed composition and closure NR); energy
results moderate confidence for the author efficiency (measured, defined
in SI, graph-read, reactor boundary only). Topsoe (technology owner)
co-authored.
