IEAGHG (2017) - fired steam methane reforming merchant H2 plant (Base Case)
Source ID: SRC004. Role: fired SMR benchmark (second reference system).
Data type: Amec Foster Wheeler engineering design with heat and material
balance; not measured plant data. IEAGHG Technical Report 2017-02.
Only the Base Case (no CO2 capture) extracted.

Scale: 100,000 Nm3/h H2 (8,994 kg/h), 99.99+% at 2.5 MPa (PSA outlet, no
compression). Natural gas 437 MW HHV (395 MW LHV), feed plus fuel. No grid
import; steam turbine exports 9.9 MWe.

Conversion: 84.6% CH4 (87.1% hydrocarbon carbon) across pre-reformer and
Terrace Wall reformer; S/C about 2.7-2.8; outlet CH4 3.4% dry.

Energy (heat basis; radiant duty stated, rest our calculations):
- Radiant duty Q_abs 96.1 MW; burner fuel Q_fired 158 MW LHV (175 MW
  HHV), mostly PSA tail gas; radiant efficiency 0.61 LHV.
- EC_CH4,abs 315 kJ/mol CH4 converted (256 per mol hydrocarbon carbon);
  EC_CH4,fired 574 kJ/mol HHV (518 LHV). Reaction enthalpy 206 kJ/mol.
- No author efficiency stated; LHV ratio 75.9% (78.4% with export power).
  Ours: eta_HHV 81.1% (83.4% with export power); f_electric 0.
- 175 MJ NG (HHV) per kg H2; 1.10 kWh exported per kg H2.

Duration: not applicable (design basis; 95% availability assumed).

System information: whole-plant boundary (H2 plant, cogen, utilities);
stream table without enthalpies; no overall energy balance. Reformer
radiant duty stated; tube count and furnace size not given. Direct CO2
0.81 kg/Nm3 H2 (about 9.0 kg/kg H2); LCOH 11.4 euro cent/Nm3 (4Q2014 EUR).

Reporting quality (R1-R4): design basis; plant boundary and LHV/HHV basis
stated; prepared by the reformer licensor (Amec Foster Wheeler) under
IEAGHG management with independent review. Confidence higher for stated
values, moderate for our derived fired duty and EC values.

Comparison with NETL (SRC003): radiant duty here vs derived process-gas
uptake there; power export here vs uncredited steam export and H2
compression there. State these boundary differences when comparing.
