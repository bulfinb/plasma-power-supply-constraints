Zhu et al. (2019) - gliding arc plasma catalytic steam methane reforming, ~250 W
Source ID: SRC012. Catal. Today 337, 69-75; ESI Figs. S1-S3. Experimental results only.

Scale: Plasma scale 149-246 W DC discharge power (resistor-corrected V x I).
Feed 2.5-3 SLM CH4+steam (CH4 ~0.034-0.037 mol/min), S/C 2-3. Insulated
reactor with no extra heating; Ni/CeO2/Al2O3 bed after the arc. Steam from
an external vaporizer (duty not reported; reviewer indicative ~78 W, ~1/3
of P). Ballast-resistor and supply losses not reported.

Conversion: plasma alone 40-65% CH4; with the catalyst 90% CH4 and 42% H2O
at the same 246 W (S/C 3, 3 SLM), close to equilibrium at the 651 degC bed
exit. Catalyst removes C2Hx and shifts CO to CO2. C and H balances ~100 +/-
3%. Bed temperature 977 to 651 degC, set by plasma heat alone.

Energy (power basis = discharge power; vaporizer heat excluded):
- EC_CH4 490 kJ/mol with catalyst vs 679 plasma alone at the same power;
  594-601 for plasma alone at S/C 2. ~646 with the indicative steam duty.
- EC_H2 ~144 kJ/mol, EY_H2 ~50 g/kWh, eta_P,LHV ~75% with catalyst
  (H2 reconstructed from selectivities).
- Author metric: efficiency of CH4 to "total H2" (H2 + CO), 75% with
  catalyst vs 59% plasma alone; energy cost 1.5 vs 2.3 kWh/Nm3 t-H2.
  Reproduced within ~2 points.
- eta_HHV (partial; vaporizer and losses excluded) ~76% with catalyst.

Duration: 6 h steady at the optimum (ESI Fig. S2); catalyst reduced in
situ, unchanged element distribution after the test.

System information: lab-scale insulated reactor; two GCs with internal
standards. Plasma gas temperature, wall power, vaporizer duty, repeats
and costs not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results higher confidence (internal
standards, closed C/H balances, matched plasma-alone control, equilibrium
comparison, 6 h test); energy results moderate confidence (discharge
power measured, but steam generation ~1/3 of P and ballast losses omitted;
author efficiency counts CO as H2).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 2-5 (printed 70-73), Table 1, Figs. 1-5;
ESI.pdf pp. 2-3; SRC012-E001-E021. Calculations SRC012-C001-C005,
