Akande and Lee (2022) - steam methane reforming in a 915 MHz microwave plasma torch, 15-50 kW
Source ID: SRC008. Int. J. Hydrogen Energy 47, 2874-2884, with corrigendum (47, 25876).
Experimental results only; ASPEN Plus simulations excluded. No catalyst.

CAUTION: the headline results are internally inconsistent. The reported
2247 g H2/h and 70 g H2/kWh at 32 kW are 1.7 times the stoichiometric
maximum for the stated 60 lpm CH4 (1295 g/h; 40.5 g/kWh). The corrigendum
does not address this; its completed outlet composition implies 83% CH4
conversion, not the 96.6% reported. Do not use the H2 rate or energy
yield as demonstrated performance.

Scale: Plasma scale 15-50 kW absorbed microwave power (incident minus
reflected; 75 kW generator rating). CH4 60 lpm (~2.7 mol/min) with 180-360
lpm steam from a separate steam generator (duty not reported; reviewer
indicative estimate 4-6 kW at 180 lpm). Reformer 0.28 m x 2.27 m (~140 L),
500-650 degC gradient. Generator wall power not reported.

Conversion (as reported): 74.6% at 17 kW rising to 97.7% at 47 kW (S/C 3);
96.6-99.8% at 32 kW for S/C 3-5. Conflicts: corrigendum composition gives
83%, and Fig. 10 outlet CH4 fractions imply about 30-90% across 27-42 kW.
No soot observed (qualitative).

Energy (power basis = absorbed microwave power; steam generation excluded):
- Synthesis headline (BB, 2026-11-26): 835 kJ/mol at 83.1% (corrigendum
  composition, carbon basis, no soot; OP008), not the reported 96.6%.
- EC_CH4 511 kJ/mol (17 kW) to 1078 (47 kW); 742 at the 32 kW author
  optimum, or ~835 if conversion is the 83% implied by the composition.
- EC_H2/EY_H2 from the corrigendum composition (carbon-balance flows):
  ~220 kJ/mol, ~33 g H2/kWh, eta_P,LHV ~67%. Author H2 rate not used.
- Author metric: energy yield = H2 rate / absorbed MW power, max 70
  g/kWh at 32 kW (exceeds the stoichiometric limit, see above).
- eta_HHV: partial only, ~66% at the composition point (steam generation
  and generator losses excluded); lower confidence.

Duration: not reported. "Steady plasma flame" and start-up under 10 min
are qualitative claims.

System information: torch, reformer and heat exchanger tested; separator,
compressor and PSA shown but not used. Outlet flow measurement, analyser
calibration, balances, steam-generator duty, generator efficiency,
lifetime and costs not reported. Corresponding author also affiliated
with Green Science Corporation.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results lower confidence (H2 rate
above stoichiometric limit; three conflicting conversion indications);
energy results lower confidence (absorbed power measured properly, but
energy yield depends on the impossible H2 rate; steam heat unreported).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 4-9 (printed 2877-2882), Figs. 6-10;
erratum.pdf p. 1; SRC008-E001-E030. Calculations SRC008-C001-C007,
E041-E048; outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
