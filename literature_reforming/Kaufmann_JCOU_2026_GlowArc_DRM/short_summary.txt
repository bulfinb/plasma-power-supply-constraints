Kaufmann et al. (2026) - dry reforming in a rotating DC glow-to-arc discharge with big gap, ~0.5-1.7 kW
Source ID: SRC027. J. CO2 Util. 105, 103347 (open access). Univ. Stuttgart; no ESI.
Experimental. No catalyst. Pure-CO2 and pure-CH4 runs out of scope (not extracted).

Scale: 6 SLM CO2/CH4 (50/50 or 60/40), no diluent; 0.11-0.13 mol/min CH4.
Plasma ~540-1430 W at the selected points (SEI x flow); supply 400-1750 W
(text; 1660 W plasma quoted at 40 mm). Single pin-ring discharge, gap
20-40 mm, rotated by 21 mT magnets. Power-electronic current control, no
ballast: plasma/supply 88-94%. Supply = DC supply output; not wall plug.

Conversion: X_tot 40-68%, rising with gap and power. 50/50 at 40 mm:
X_CH4 ~66%, X_CO2 ~70% (OP002). Carbon deposits at 50/50 (S_C,CO ~81%);
60/40 suppresses it (S_C,CO ~93-96%).

Energy (power basis; no external heat; 22.414 L/mol assumed):
- EC_CH4 574 (OP001, ~540 W) to 971 kJ/mol (OP002, ~1430 W) at the plasma;
  640-1080 at the supply. 60/40 OP003 1178 (EC_CH4 penalises CO2-rich feed).
- +9% at 24.46 L/mol (SLM reference not stated).
- Author EC 3.3-4.7 eV per CO2+CH4 converted (reproduced as 3.1-4.9);
  eta_BGs (LHV) max 68% (60/40), 60% (50/50) on plasma power.
- Text 1660 W vs ~1430 W from SEI x flow at the top point, unresolved.

Duration: 2-5 min per CO2/CH4 run; no long-term test.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence (bar-chart
readings, alpha correlation, short runs); energy results moderate confidence
(two measured boundaries; SLM reference and a power mismatch unresolved).
