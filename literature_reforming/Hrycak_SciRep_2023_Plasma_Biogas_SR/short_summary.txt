Hrycak et al. (2023) - steam reforming of synthetic biogas in a 915 MHz microwave plasma, 4.5-6.5 kW
Source ID: SRC010. Sci. Rep. 13, 2204 (open access). Experimental results only;
10 kW extrapolation excluded. No catalyst. No ESI located.

Scale: Plasma scale 4.5-6.5 kW absorbed microwave power. CH4:CO2 3000-12,000
NL/h (40-100% CH4; 1.1-3.4 mol/min CH4 at the extracted points) plus 2-3
kg/h steam from an electric induction vaporizer (energy not reported;
reviewer indicative estimate 1.7-2.5 kW, 25-38% of the microwave power).
Generator wall power not reported.

Conversion: CH4 49-74% at 6.5 kW (Table 1); 40-49% across 4.5-6.5 kW at
6000 NL/h, 50% CH4. Best H2 rate 239 g/h (75% CH4, 6000 NL/h, conversion
49%). H2 rate and conversion are consistent, but our carbon check closes
at only 66-73% (authors report none; possible unquantified soot). Water
not measured.

Energy (power basis = absorbed microwave power; steam generation excluded):
- EC_CH4 238 kJ/mol (OP001) to 471 (OP003); 299-354 in the power sweep.
  With the indicative steam duty, ~330-590 kJ/mol. Near the reaction
  enthalpy, so treat as lower bounds (unreported steam heat; carbon gap).
- EC_H2 197-275 kJ/mol; EY_H2 26-37 g/kWh; eta_P,LHV 38-57%.
- Author metric: energy yield = H2 rate / absorbed microwave power, best
  36.8 g/kWh; also a new "energy-CH4 feedstock consumption yield".
- eta_HHV (partial; vaporizer and generator excluded) 23-48%.

Duration: not reported; stability only described as soot-free operation.

System information: microwave source, quartz tube, gas-soot separator; N2
flow marker for outlet flows. Temperatures, water, solid carbon, balances,
vaporizer energy, lifetime and costs not reported.

Reporting quality: conversion/product results moderate confidence
(tabulated matched points, flow marker, consistent H2 and conversion;
carbon closure 66-73%, no temperatures); energy results lower confidence
(steam vaporizer energy unreported; carbon gap may flatter EC_CH4).

Traceability: manuscript.pdf pp. 3-12, Tables 1-3, Figs. 4-9;
SRC010-E001-E026. Calculations SRC010-C001-C005, E041-E048;
outputs/calculations.py. Details: outputs/extraction.md and
outputs/evidence.csv.
